NOTESphotopipe — assumptions & design system
Assumptions
  • Single-window desktop app, 1440×900 baseline. No responsive work.
  • Onboard cyan is the only accent, and only on chrome — never on a photo surface.
  • Photos render on neutral greys (#121414 / #1A1C1C wells) in both themes.
  • Dark is the working default for photo screens; light is shown for library/setup screens.
Decision semantics
Keep — Space
Reject — X
Keeper — K (best of a duplicate group)
Undecided — U to clear
Cursor — neutral, never colored
Defect flags are monochrome

Flags are facts, not decisions, so they carry no hue — white glyphs on a black scrim over the frame. Codes stay two or three characters so a tile never needs more than one chip.

BLR BF OE UE IQA
Nav shell

54-px Onboard rail: Libraries → Review → Duplicates → Export → Develop. Develop is drawn but disabled with a dot marker — the slot for RAW→JPEG after export.
